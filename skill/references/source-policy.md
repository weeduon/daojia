# Source and provenance policy

## Contents

## Source priority

Use this order:

1. Primary text images, historical editions, catalog records, and formal institutional rules.
2. Reliable critical editions, academic monographs, and peer-reviewed research.
3. Public explanations from identifiable Daoist institutions or practitioners, scoped to their tradition.
4. General reference works and educational sites.
5. Anonymous posts, short videos, sales pages, or unattributed “secret methods”.

Do not use level 5 material as factual proof.

## Claim construction

For important claims, preserve:

- title;
- section, chapter, scroll, or page;
- author/attribution when known;
- edition or transcription source;
- tradition and approximate period;
- stable URL or image anchor;
- whether the wording was checked against an image;
- whether the conclusion is a quotation, paraphrase, or inference.

Do not write “the Daoist canon says” when only one late text, local manual, or modern webpage supports the claim.

## Text variants

When exact characters matter:

1. inspect the image edition when available;
2. report missing or uncertain characters;
3. keep variant characters distinct when they change meaning;
4. do not normalize a transcription silently;
5. compare another edition before claiming a quotation is definitive.

If a site uses placeholders such as `◇`, state that the underlying character is missing or uncertain.

## Copyright and quotation

Do not bundle or reproduce an entire third-party website without a clear license or the rights holder's permission. Store bibliographic metadata, links, original summaries, and short necessary quotations. For ancient public-domain texts, still respect the copyright in modern editions, punctuation, annotations, translations, images, and database arrangement.

When the user supplies authorized local source files, use those files as the primary corpus and preserve their provenance.

## Quality labels

- `image-checked`: wording verified against a page image.
- `site-checked`: the site marks the transcription as 已校对/校对一次.
- `catalog-only`: title and category confirmed, full text not inspected.
- `cross-checked`: compared with an independent edition or reliable source.
- `uncertain`: unresolved variant, attribution, or provenance problem.

The site's own 已校对 label is useful but is not independent academic peer review.
